# Credits & Open-Source Attributions

The KAKA'S COACHING INSTITUTE (KCI) Educational Platform utilizes the following open-source assets and libraries:

- **Kokoro-82M TTS Model:** Open-weights text-to-speech model released under the [Apache-2.0 License](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M). Used for offline generation of British English educational audio prompts.
- **Twemoji / Inline SVG Artwork:** Vector artwork styled after Twemoji graphics, licensed under [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) by Twitter, Inc. and contributors.
- **Lucide Icons:** Open-source iconography licensed under the [ISC License](https://lucide.dev/license).
- **Google Fonts:**
  - `Fredoka` & `Nunito` under the [SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org/).
  - `Andika` under the [SIL Open Font License](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL) — specially designed for clear literacy and beginning readers.
- **Libraries:**
  - GSAP (GreenSock) animation engine.
  - Chart.js (MIT License) for visual score summary.
  - Lenis smooth scrolling (MIT License).
  - html2canvas & jsPDF for student certificate export.
